Polish the Batch mode chrome. A range preview now draws in a gutter that every row always reserves, so the mark box, tree and label keep their columns whether or not a range is open. The heading and the key hints each get a blank line of their own, the filter controls show their default or active state by colour, and the hints are grouped by purpose in Pi's hint style — one group per line, labels in a shared column, whole hints dropped from the end of a group instead of cut in half on a narrow terminal. Keys and behavior are unchanged.
